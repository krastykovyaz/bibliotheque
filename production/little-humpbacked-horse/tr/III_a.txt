Zealously adding wood,
Talking about Ivan
Quietly among themselves,
And laughing now and then.

And bathe in the cauldrons, brother!”
[N] The Tsar cried to Ivan.
Then Ivan took off his clothes,
Not answering a word.
And the young queen,
Not to see his nakedness,
Wrapped herself in her veil.
Ivan went up to the cauldrons,
Looked in, and scratched himself.
[TSAR] “Why have you stopped, Ivan?”
[N] Cried the Tsar again.
[TSAR] “Do what you must, brother!”
[IVAN] “Could you not, your Grace,

I drank mead, wine and beer;
